# SEO and AI Discovery

Implemented: semantic landmarks/headings, unique title and description, canonical, robots directive, Open Graph, Twitter card, favicon, manifest, generated sitemap, `robots.txt`, `llms.txt`, crawlable section copy, descriptive internal anchors, and JSON-LD for Organization, WebSite, and Service.

Core content and the no-JavaScript Solution Finder fallback are present in static HTML. Copy explicitly describes the entity, services, audiences, problems, decision process, and intended outcomes without keyword stuffing.

Not implemented intentionally: Article and identifiable Case Study schema. No reviewed article or permission-cleared case exists, so adding those schemas would create false publication/claim signals. Add them only with their corresponding real page and evidence.

Deploy Preview indexing must be verified in Netlify. The production canonical/domain assumption is `https://intuise.com/`.
